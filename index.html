Systems Reference Notes

Scope

This page collects evergreen engineering reference notes: how small, well-instrumented systems are documented, measured, and kept honest over time.

Method

Write the claim down, name the instrument that could prove it wrong, and record the reading. A result without a measurement path is an opinion and is labelled as one.

Maintenance

Notes are revised when the underlying practice changes, not on a schedule. Dates below change only when the text above them does.
