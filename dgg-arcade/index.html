Classic Games — Sourcing and Maintenance Notes

Sourcing

Classic game software is sourced from legitimate channels only: original media, publisher re-releases, and public-domain or openly licensed releases. Provenance is recorded per title.

Maintenance

Cartridges and discs are cleaned, imaged where the law and the licence allow, and stored dry. Emulation notes here concern preservation of lawfully owned material.
