Forecast Scoreboard Notes

How scoring works

Each forecast is written down in advance with a probability and a resolution date. When the date arrives, the outcome is scored against the record as written — no quiet edits, no retroactive wording changes.

Reading the board

A forecaster who is right 75% of the time on 75% forecasts is calibrated, not lucky. The notes here track calibration over time, in public, including the misses.
