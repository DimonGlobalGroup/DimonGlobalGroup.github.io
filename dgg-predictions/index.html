Methodology

RULE 01

Logged before

Every forecast is logged BEFORE the event, with a probability and a resolution rule. A forecast that was not written down in advance does not count, and does not appear here.

RULE 02

Append-only

The ledger is append-only — never edited after logging. Wording, probabilities, and resolution rules stay exactly as first recorded, including the forecasts the desk gets wrong.

RULE 03

Brier-scored

Resolved forecasts are scored with the Brier score. Lower is better. The score is published with the resolution, whether it flatters the desk or not.

RULE 04

Calibration-checked

Forecasts are also checked for calibration: in each probability bucket, the observed frequency should approach the stated probability over time.

Brier score = mean of (probability − outcome)² — where the outcome is 1 if the forecast came true and 0 if it did not. Lower is better: a perfect forecast scores 0, and always saying 50% scores 0.25.

Calibration is the second test. Group forecasts by their stated probability — all the 30% calls, all the 75% calls — and, in each probability bucket, the observed frequency should approach the stated probability. A desk that says 75% should be right about three times in four, no more and no less.

The beats

The desk follows a narrow set of public, forecastable signals — events with clear outcomes, public reporting, and a date by which the answer is known. That discipline is the point: if an event cannot be resolved cleanly, it is not forecast here.

The desk publishes forecasts and their grades, nothing else; no security is ever recommended here.
