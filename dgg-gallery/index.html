Dimon Global Group — Gallery

The moments the firm
must not forget, hung as art.

The Group keeps a gallery of memory frames — works of art that mark the moments the firm must not forget. What hangs here are the public exhibits of that record: three frames, each with its plaque, each earned before it was exhibited. There are no image files in this gallery; each work is rendered here as a composed plate, and described in words on its plaque.

Plate I · Lamp & brass on dark wood
EXHIBIT I

The Founding Night

November 1, 2026

The artworkA dark room lit by one desk lamp; on the desk, a brass nameplate engraved and not yet mounted.

The plaqueOn this night the firm took its name — Dimon Global Group — its domain was registered, and its one standard was set: truth, made real. Nothing in the room is finished, and that is the point of the frame. Seven brass rivets run along the frame's lower edge.

Plate II · Balance & columns of light
EXHIBIT II

The Verified Record

November 1, 2026

The artworkA balance scale whose two pans hold columns of light; one column rises to exactly the beam’s mark.

The plaqueNEUROGEN/SPHERION v0.4 was measured, not asserted — task accuracy 1.000 against the 0.830 baseline, reproducibility 100%, and the release hash-verified before it shipped. The scale does not tip toward hope; it rests where the evidence rests. A single green thread runs through the weave at the lower-left corner.

Plate III · Lit windows above dark water
EXHIBIT III

The Constellation

November 2, 2026

The artworkA small city of lit windows above dark water — the pages lit one by one: flagship, systems, media, founder, cinema.

The plaqueThis was the night the constellation lit — one firm, many rooms, each with its own work, reflected on the same dark water. No window was lit for decoration; each one is a room where something is actually made. In the painting, the third window from the left is lit violet; all the rest are green.
